THE AVENTRAXO METHOD

Simple for the client. Structured behind the scenes.

Every project starts with a clear brief and finishes with a quality-controlled delivery.

01

Define

We agree on the target, fields, sources, format and acceptance criteria.

02

Research

Information is collected against the agreed criteria, not guessed or filled blindly.

03

Verify

Records are reviewed for consistency, duplicates and obvious source errors.

04

Deliver

The finished research is organized into the format your team can use.
